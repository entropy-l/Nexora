Nexora

Rules, Regulations & Participant Agreement

Official Competition Guidelines | November 2026

1. Introduction

Nexora is a Shark Tank-inspired innovation and entrepreneurship competition hosted at school. It provides participants with an opportunity to develop, present and defend their ideas in front of a judging panel.

The competition focuses on creativity, problem-solving, innovation, technical ability and the practical execution of ideas.

By participating in Nexora, all participants agree to follow the rules and regulations outlined in this document.

2. Time Limit

Each participant will be allocated exactly 60 minutes for their assigned competition session.

  • Participants must complete their work within the allocated time.
  • The timer begins when the participant's session officially starts.
  • Extensions will not be granted unless explicitly approved by the organisers.
  • Participants must manage their time responsibly.
  • Any work presented must comply with the competition's originality and AI policies.

Participants are expected to make effective use of the available time rather than focus on unnecessary features or incomplete ideas.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy

Nexora recognises that artificial intelligence can be a useful tool for improving productivity. Therefore, limited AI assistance is permitted.

Permitted AI Usage:

  • Brainstorming ideas and exploring possible approaches.
  • Understanding programming concepts and technical documentation.
  • Debugging code written by the participant.
  • Explaining errors and suggesting improvements.
  • Improving workflow efficiency and productivity.
  • Researching general concepts and learning unfamiliar technologies.

Prohibited AI Usage:

  • Using AI to develop the entire project or application.
  • Asking AI to generate the complete project and submitting it as your own work.
  • Having AI independently implement the core functionality of your project.
  • Using AI-generated complete solutions as a substitute for your own development.
  • Misrepresenting AI-generated work as independently developed work.

STRICT ENFORCEMENT: Participants caught using AI for full or substantial project development during the competition will be immediately disqualified.

There will be NO exceptions to this rule. Claims of time pressure, convenience or lack of technical knowledge will not be accepted as justification.

AI is intended to assist participants, not replace their creativity, problem-solving or development efforts.

4. Originality & Ownership

All participants are expected to demonstrate genuine involvement in the creation of their projects.

  • Projects must represent the participant's own effort and understanding.
  • Copying another participant's project or submitting someone else's work is prohibited.
  • Existing open-source libraries, frameworks and tools may be used where appropriate.
  • Participants must be able to explain the technologies and major components used in their projects.
  • Previously existing work must not be falsely presented as something developed entirely during Nexora.

5. Project Development

Participants may develop any idea that is appropriate for the competition and can be reasonably demonstrated.

Projects may include software applications, websites, technical solutions, business concepts, prototypes or other innovative ideas.

  • Participants should focus on solving a real or clearly defined problem.
  • Projects should have a clear purpose and intended audience.
  • Participants should prioritise functionality and meaningful execution over unnecessary complexity.
  • Any external resources, APIs, frameworks or libraries used should be acknowledged when relevant.

6. School Environment & Conduct

Nexora will take place during official school hours. All participants are expected to maintain appropriate behaviour throughout the competition.

  • Participants must follow instructions given by organisers and supervising teachers.
  • Participants must not disrupt other competitors.
  • Unauthorised collaboration, sharing of solutions or interference with another participant's work is prohibited.
  • Participants must respect school equipment, facilities and resources.
  • Any attempt to gain an unfair advantage may result in disqualification.

7. Internet & External Resources

Participants may use the internet for legitimate research, documentation and accessing development resources, subject to school restrictions and organiser instructions.

Using online resources to copy complete solutions, obtain another participant's work or bypass competition rules is prohibited.

8. Presentation & Pitching

Participants must be prepared to present their ideas and explain their projects to the judging panel.

A presentation may cover the following:

  • The problem being addressed.
  • The proposed solution and how it works.
  • The target audience and potential users.
  • What makes the idea innovative or different.
  • The technologies and methods used.
  • Potential applications, feasibility and future improvements.

Participants should be prepared to demonstrate their work and answer questions from the judges.

9. Judging Criteria

Projects may be evaluated using the following criteria:

  • Innovation: Originality and creativity of the idea.
  • Problem Solving: How effectively the project addresses its intended problem.
  • Execution: Quality of implementation and functionality.
  • Feasibility: Practicality and potential for real-world use.
  • Presentation: Clarity, communication and ability to defend the idea.
  • Understanding: The participant's knowledge of their own project.

The judging panel's decisions will be considered final.

10. Disqualification

The organisers reserve the right to disqualify participants who violate competition rules.

Grounds for disqualification include:

  • Prohibited AI usage, including full AI-based project development.
  • Plagiarism or submission of another person's work.
  • Deliberate misrepresentation of project development.
  • Unauthorised collaboration or unfair assistance.
  • Intentional disruption of the competition.
  • Failure to follow instructions from organisers or supervising authorities.

Violations of the AI policy involving full or substantial AI-based development will result in immediate elimination without exceptions.

11. Organiser Authority

The organisers and supervising teachers reserve the right to interpret these rules, monitor competition activities, investigate suspected violations and take appropriate action.

Any situation not explicitly covered in this document will be addressed by the organisers. Participants are expected to cooperate with any reasonable verification of their work.

12. Project Demonstration & Technical Issues

Participants are responsible for ensuring that their projects are ready for demonstration.

Technical issues, software failures or incomplete implementations should be communicated to the organisers. Such issues will be considered at the discretion of the judging panel and will not automatically guarantee additional time.

13. Final Agreement

Participation in Nexora indicates acceptance of all competition rules and regulations.

Participants acknowledge that they are responsible for the originality of their work, their use of AI tools, their conduct and their adherence to the allocated time limit.

The purpose of Nexora is to encourage independent thinking, innovation, entrepreneurship and practical problem-solving. Participants are expected to compete fairly and demonstrate their own abilities.

14. Acknowledgement

Failure to comply with these rules may result in penalties, including immediate disqualification.
